HTD groove profile attribution

HTD 3M and 5M sampled tooth-groove coordinates are adapted from the
Parametric Pulley with multiple belt profiles by droftarts (January 2012),
as preserved in rbuckland's OpenSCAD parametric-pulley source:
https://github.com/rbuckland/openscad.parametric-pulley
https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:16627

The original model is distributed under Creative Commons Attribution-
ShareAlike. The adapted profile data and generated pulley designs are
provided under CC BY-SA 3.0. Attribution must be retained when sharing.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

Adaptations: circular-arc fitting (under 0.002 mm deviation from sampled
coordinates), JavaScript profile extrusion, circular lands, hex shaft
bores, adjustable flanges, tube inserts, and bearing cones.
This is a sampled community profile, not a certified cutter profile.
Verify belt fit and shaft clearance with a test part before use.
